Media Search – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Media Search is a browser extension that lets you search text selected on a web page on websites you choose. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your search buttons, interface language, font, logo, hotkey and site list are saved in your browser (chrome.storage.local). This data never leaves your device and is not sent to the developer. You can export or delete it at any time on the settings page, or by removing the extension.

Selected text

The extension reads the text you select on a page only to perform the action you request:

  • Search buttons, context menu, Google button: the selected text is placed in the address of the website you chose (for example a movie database or Google) and opened in a new tab. That website's own privacy policy applies from that point.
  • Translate button: only when you press it, the selected text is sent over HTTPS to the Google Translate service (translate.googleapis.com) to obtain a translation. Google's privacy policy applies to that request. Nothing is sent to the developer.
  • Copy button: copies the text to your clipboard locally.

The extension does not read, store or transmit any other page content, browsing history or personal information, and it does not use cookies or identifiers.

Fonts

The popup and settings pages load font files from Google Fonts. Google may receive your IP address with that request.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, share or transfer user data to third parties, except the translation request described above, which you trigger yourself.

Limited Use disclosure

The use of information received from Chrome APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Selected text is used only to provide the extension's single purpose (searching and translating the text you select). It is not transferred to anyone except through the translation request you trigger yourself, it is not used or transferred for advertising, personalized or interest-based ads, or credit decisions, and it is not read by humans.
